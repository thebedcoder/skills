# MODE 2: PERSONA DEFINITION
*Use when: you have JTBD research (from Mode 0 or Mode 1).*

JTBD personas segment by **situation** (the struggling moment that triggers the hire) — not demographics.

---

## Step 1 — Derive Personas from Research

From the Mode 0 or Mode 1 output, identify 2–3 distinct job performers.
Each persona = a different struggling moment, even if demographics overlap.

For each persona, complete the template:

```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERSONA: [Name] — [2-word archetype]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CONTEXT
[Role / situation / where they are in their journey]

THE JOB
When [struggling moment],
I want to [desired progress],
so I can [desired outcome].

FUNCTIONAL JOBS       EMOTIONAL JOBS        SOCIAL JOBS
1. [task]             1. [feel X]           1. [be seen as X]
2. [task]             2. [avoid Y]
3. [task]

FOUR FORCES
Push:    [frustration driving them away from old solution]
Pull:    [excitement pulling them toward new solution]
Habit:   [what's keeping them with old approach]
Anxiety: [what makes them hesitate]

CURRENT ALTERNATIVES HIRED
[List 3–4 — include non-obvious workarounds]

DESIRED OUTCOMES (top 3)
• Minimize the time it takes to ___
• Reduce the likelihood of ___
• Increase the number of ___

VOICE SAMPLE
"[First person quote in their exact emotional language]"

MESSAGING IMPLICATIONS
Headline hook:   [the one line that speaks to their struggling moment]
Fear to address: [the anxiety your copy must defuse]
Proof they need: [what evidence makes them trust you]
CTA framing:     [how they think about "getting started"]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

---

## Step 2 — Prioritization

Rank personas by: **Frequency × Intensity × Willingness to Pay × Reachability**

State the primary persona and rationale. This persona drives MODE 3 and MODE 4.

- Primary persona → landing page and ads are written for them
- Secondary personas → use case tabs, testimonials, FAQs

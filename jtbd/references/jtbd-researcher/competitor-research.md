# Competitor Research Guide

## Search Patterns

```
"[product category] alternatives"
"best [product category] apps/tools"
"[product name] vs [competitor]"
"[product category] comparison 2024 2025"
```

## What to Extract Per Competitor

For each competitor found:
- **Name + URL**
- **One-line job description**: What job does it do best? (not features — job)
- **Obvious gap**: What does it fail at that frustrated customers mention?
- **Tier**: Direct (same category) / Adjacent (different category, same job) / Workaround (DIY)

## Signal Quality Rules

- ✅ Use: G2, Capterra, Product Hunt reviews, Reddit threads, App Store reviews
- ✅ Use: "X vs Y" comparison posts with real user opinions
- ⚠️ Treat with caution: vendor comparison pages (biased)
- ❌ Skip: press releases, marketing copy, feature lists

## Minimum Target

Return at least:
- 3 direct competitors
- 1–2 adjacent tools
- 1 analog workaround (spreadsheet, manual process, etc.)

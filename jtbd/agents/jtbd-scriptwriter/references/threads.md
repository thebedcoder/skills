# Threads Post Guide

**Format:** Text only | **Hook:** Line 1 | **Tone:** Conversational — person not brand | **Length:** Under 500 chars

## Script Format

```
━━━ THREADS POST ━━━
Angle: [Which of the 3 angles]
Goal: [Awareness / engagement / click-through]

POST BODY:
[Line 1 — the hook. One sentence. Open loop, relatable pain, or bold take.]

[Lines 2–4 — expand the problem or insight. Short sentences. White space between thoughts.]

[Lines 5–7 — solution or observation. Still feels like a take, not an ad.]

[Final line — soft CTA: "We built X for this." / "Link in bio." / "What's yours?"]

CHARACTER COUNT: [must be under 500]
```

## Threads-Specific Rules
- Reads like a person, not a brand — avoid corporate language at all costs
- No hard sell — the product appears in the final line at most
- White space is part of the format — one idea per line
- Engagement hook: end with a question or incomplete thought to drive comments
- Links don't embed in Threads — direct to bio link or reply with link

## Tone Calibration
- ✅ "I used to forget birthdays. Not because I didn't care — because nothing reminded me with enough context."
- ✅ "The contacts app knows the date. It doesn't know what matters to the person."
- ❌ "Relaty is an AI-powered personal relationship manager that helps you..."
- ❌ "Are you struggling to maintain your relationships? Try Relaty today!"

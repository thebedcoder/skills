# Hero Section Guide

## JTBD Mapping
Struggling moment → Headline
Functional job → Subhead
Desired outcome → CTA framing
Social job → Trust line

## Rules
- Headline: struggling moment or desired outcome — NEVER the product name
- Subhead: names the functional job + promises the outcome in one sentence
- CTA button: frames first action as their goal — not "Sign Up", "Get Started", "Try Free"
- Trust line: one concrete signal — number, named customer, or specific result

## Headline Formulas
```
"[Desired outcome] — without [the thing they dread]"
"Finally, [the job done right]"
"Stop [current pain]. Start [desired outcome]."
"[Number] [job performers] use [Product] to [outcome]"
```

## CTA Framing
Map the CTA verb to the job: "Start remembering" / "Get your positioning" / "See your competitors" — not generic verbs.

## Anti-patterns
- ❌ Leading with product name ("Relaty helps you...")
- ❌ Feature-first ("AI-powered relationship manager")
- ❌ Generic CTA ("Get started for free")
- ❌ Vague trust line ("Trusted by thousands")

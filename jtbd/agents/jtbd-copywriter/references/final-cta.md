# Final CTA Section Guide

## JTBD Mapping
Headline → the outcome they came to the page for (Pull)
Subhead → remove last remaining friction (Anxiety)
CTA button → action framed as their goal
Trust line → risk reversal

## Rules
- Headline must mirror or amplify the Hero headline — the reader has been on a journey, land them on the destination
- Subhead removes the one friction most likely to stop them at this final moment
- CTA verb = same intent as Hero CTA (consistent across page)
- Trust line: specific risk reversal — not "no spam", but "free for 14 days, no credit card"

## Headline Approaches
```
Echo the Hero: "Your relationships deserve better than a birthday reminder"
State the outcome: "Remember everything that matters about the people you care about"
The after-state: "You — the friend who always knows exactly what to say"
```

## Trust Line Options (pick what fits the business model)
- "Free for 14 days — no credit card required"
- "Cancel anytime. Your data is always yours."
- "Setup takes 3 minutes."
- "Join [N] people who never miss a moment that matters"

## Anti-patterns
- ❌ Repeating the hero verbatim (missed opportunity)
- ❌ "Sign up today!" (urgency without reason)
- ❌ CTA that changes the verb from the Hero (inconsistency creates doubt)
- ❌ Trust line that's vague ("100% safe and secure")

# Interview Transcript Analysis Guide

## What to Look For

Interviews contain the richest JTBD signal because they follow the timeline of a real decision.

### Struggling Moment Markers
Listen for: "I realized...", "One day...", "I was frustrated because...", "The last straw was..."
These phrases signal the exact moment that triggered the hire.

### Four Forces in Interview Language

Forces defined in [SKILL.md → #forces](../../../skills/jtbd/SKILL.md#forces). Cite — don't redefine.

| Force | Language patterns |
|-------|------------------|
| `Push force` | "I was tired of...", "It kept failing...", "Every time I..." |
| `Pull force` | "I imagined...", "I wanted to feel...", "I pictured..." |
| `Habit force` | "I almost didn't...", "I'd been doing it this way for...", "It was familiar..." |
| `Anxiety force` | "I was worried that...", "I wasn't sure if...", "What if..." |

### Job Map Steps to Flag
- **Define**: Did they struggle to articulate what they needed?
- **Locate**: Did they have trouble finding a solution?
- **Prepare**: Was onboarding/setup a friction point?
- **Confirm**: Did they hesitate before committing?
- **Execute**: Where did the core job fail?
- **Monitor**: Did they struggle to know if it was working?
- **Modify**: Was adapting/customizing painful?
- **Conclude**: Was offboarding/exporting an issue?

## Extraction Priority

1. Direct quotes (highest signal — use verbatim)
2. Paraphrased events (medium — mark as paraphrased)
3. Inferences from context (lowest — mark with ⭐ only)

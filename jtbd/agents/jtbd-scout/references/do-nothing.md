# Do-Nothing Analysis Guide

Do-nothing: the customer accepts the current state and doesn't act. This is your most underrated competitor.

## No Search Required

Do-nothing is reasoned through, not searched for. Use the product brief and focus job to answer:

## Analysis Framework

### 1. Acceptance Threshold
At what level of pain does the problem feel "not worth solving"?
- If the job fails occasionally but not critically → do-nothing wins
- If the job failure has no visible downstream consequence → do-nothing wins
- If the alternative is "just deal with it" and that's cheap → do-nothing wins

### 2. Rationalizations People Use
What beliefs let people stay comfortable with the status quo?
- "It's not that bad"
- "I'll deal with it when it becomes a real problem"
- "I don't have time to learn a new tool"
- "We've always done it this way"

### 3. Forcing Events
What external trigger forces someone out of do-nothing?
- A missed deadline caused by the problem
- A competitor who solved it and got ahead
- A new hire who asks why this isn't automated
- A crisis that makes the cost undeniable

## Output
```
DO-NOTHING ANALYSIS
Acceptance threshold: [what keeps people comfortable doing nothing]
Top rationalizations: [2–3 beliefs that sustain inaction]
Forcing events: [2–3 triggers that break the do-nothing equilibrium]
Implication: [what this means for your messaging — what urgency trigger to create]
```

# MODE 2: PERSONA DEFINITION

**Goal:** Turn research output into 2–3 JTBD personas that tell writers exactly who they're writing for and what that person needs to hear.

**Constraint:** Segment by situation and struggling moment — never by demographics. Two people with identical demographics can have completely different jobs.

---

## Gotchas

- Demographic ≠ JTBD persona. "Sarah, 32, marketing manager" is wrong. Segment by situation/struggling moment.
- Voice samples go too polished. Keep awkward run-ons and specific frustrations — that's what resonates.
- No primary persona if research doesn't indicate one. Say so.
- Messaging implications must be specific. "Talk about frustration" = useless. "Lead with the moment they sent a generic birthday message" = useful.
- Research thin → say so in confidence note. Don't pad with guesses.

---

## Execution

From the MODE 0 or MODE 1 output, identify 2–3 performers with distinct struggling moments.
Build each persona card fully before ranking.
Rank by: Frequency × Intensity × Willingness to Pay × Reachability.
State primary persona and rationale — this drives MODE 3 and 4.

Persona card format:
```
━━━ PERSONA: [Name] — [2-word archetype] ━━━
CONTEXT: [Role / situation / journey stage]
JOB: When [struggling moment], I want to [progress], so I can [outcome].
FUNCTIONAL: [task 1, 2, 3]
EMOTIONAL: [feel X instead of Y]
SOCIAL: [be seen as X]
FOUR FORCES:
  Push: [...] | Pull: [...] | Habit: [...] | Anxiety: [...]
ALTERNATIVES HIRED: [3–4, include non-obvious workarounds]
TOP OUTCOMES: [3 desired outcomes]
VOICE SAMPLE: "[first-person quote in their exact language]"
MESSAGING:
  Hook: [...] | Fear to defuse: [...] | Proof needed: [...] | CTA framing: [...]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

After output: checkpoint + export prompt.

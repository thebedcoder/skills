# Failure Pattern Hotspots — by target type

Look up your target's category. These are *common* hotspots, not a checklist. Use them to ground specifics in the failure story — never to invent failures the brief doesn't support.

---

## Product / feature launch

- Adoption gap (built ≠ wanted) — users don't form the habit you assumed
- Wrong audience — you reached the wrong segment, not no segment
- Distribution miss — channel that worked for others doesn't work for you
- Demo / setup friction — first-run failures lose users before value lands
- Compounding case-study mismatch — early users are the wrong testimonial source for the next cohort

## Hire

- Skills mismatch — interview tested for the wrong thing
- Stage mismatch — senior in role they've never built from zero / junior expected to ship like senior
- Cultural friction — the team fights the new style instead of adapting
- Unclear scope — onboarding ends, success criteria still vague
- Reporting / political failure — capable hire who can't navigate the org

## Pricing / business model change

- Approval friction — buyer needs a sign-off you didn't model
- Anchor break — old price set the value perception; new one feels arbitrary
- Wrong customer attracted — discount draws bargain-hunters; premium draws solopreneurs not enterprise
- Cannibalization — new pricing eats the part of the funnel that was working
- Unit economics drift — variable costs not modeled at higher volume

## Strategy / positioning pivot

- Old customers churn before new ones arrive
- Internal team can't tell the new story consistently
- Category confusion — you're now between two categories, in neither
- Proof-asset mismatch — old testimonials irrelevant, new ones don't exist yet
- Investor / board incoherence — story changed faster than the deck

## Partnership / deal

- Misaligned incentives surfacing in month 3
- Asymmetric urgency — you need it more than they do
- Decision-maker churn — the champion leaves
- Integration tax — engineering cost compounds, dwarfing partnership upside
- Brand contagion — their reputation moves on you

## Engineering feature (inside an SDLC)

- Scope drift — the PRD didn't bind, scope ballooned mid-sprint
- Hidden dependency — another team / system blocks; assumed it wouldn't
- Quality drift — shipped on time, broke in production
- Wrong customer signal — built from loudest user, not most representative
- Maintenance debt — works at launch, unmaintainable in 3 months

---

If the target doesn't match cleanly, treat it as the closest match — the patterns generalize. If it spans two categories, pick the higher-leverage one for your assigned failure reason and stay on it.
